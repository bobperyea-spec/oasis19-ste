Community Guidelines

Last updated November 22, 2026

Oasis19 is a place to find your oasis and own your feed. Keep it friendly and safe for everybody.

Not allowed (you'll be banned)

  • Hate speech or attacks on people for who they are
  • Violence, threats or encouraging harm
  • Nudity, pornography or sexual content
  • Harassment, bullying or stalking
  • Illegal activity, scams or spam
  • Sharing someone's private information
  • Pretending to be someone else

Please do

  • Be kind, even when you disagree
  • Use the report and block tools if something feels wrong
  • Keep each oasis on topic
  • Respect the people running tables and oases

Reporting

You can report a profile from that person's page. Reports go to Oasis19admin@proton.me and we review them as quickly as we can.
