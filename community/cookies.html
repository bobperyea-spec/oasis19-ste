Cookies and Local Storage

Last updated November 22, 2026

Oasis19 doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we store in your browser

  • Sign-in information, so you stay logged in (set by our authentication provider)
  • Small settings, such as whether you've seen the welcome tour
  • A cache of site files, so the app loads faster and works on slow connections

Control

You can clear this data in your browser settings at any time. Signing in again will recreate what's needed to log you in.
