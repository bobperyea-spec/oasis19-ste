Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This explains what Oasis19 collects and how it's used. We keep it simple, and we don't sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name or pen name, email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
  • Profile details you choose to add, such as a bio, profile photo and header images.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, oases, messages, dice rolls and game table activity.
  • Activity such as who you follow, which oases you join and what you report or block.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends, such as IP address and device type, used for security and keeping the site running.

How we use it

To run the site, show your content to other members, deliver messages, keep the community safe, respond to reports, and fix problems. We don't show ads based on your personal data.

Who can see what

Posts in public oases can be seen by other members. Private oases are limited to members. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, and to site administrators when needed to handle a report or a legal request.

Services we rely on

Oasis19 uses Supabase for the database, sign-in and file storage, and Namecheap for web hosting. These providers process data on our behalf. The News section links to outside publishers, which have their own privacy practices.

Sharing

We don't sell your data. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of members and the site.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete your own posts and messages, and block other members. To have your account and data deleted, email Oasis19admin@proton.me. Residents of some states and countries have additional rights, and we'll honor valid requests.

Retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service is perfectly secure.

Children

Oasis19 is for adults 18 and older. If you believe a child has created an account, please tell us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
