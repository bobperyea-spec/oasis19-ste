Accessibility Statement

Last updated November 22, 2026

Oasis19 wants everyone to be able to use the site, including people who use assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we do

  • Use headings, labels and buttons that screen readers can understand
  • Support keyboard navigation
  • Keep text readable on phones, tablets and computers, and support browser zoom
  • Avoid relying on color alone to carry meaning

Known limits

Oasis19 is a young site, and some areas may not yet fully meet these goals. This includes some game table features, such as dice animations, and user-uploaded images that may lack descriptions. We're working to improve them.

Tell us what's not working

If you have trouble using any part of Oasis19, email Oasis19admin@proton.me with the page and what happened. We'll respond and work on a fix, and we can offer the information in another way if you need it.
