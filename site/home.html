Find your people on Oasis19

Oasis19 is an online community for talking about the things you enjoy, from Chicago Bears football and home cooking to running a small business. You can join the groups that interest you, swap ideas with other members, and read along without ads or an algorithm deciding which conversations you see.

Find an oasis you’ll enjoy

We call our topic groups oases. In Chicago Bears, you can talk about Sunday’s game, while Food and Drink is a place to share a recipe you’ve made enough times to know it by heart. Small Business Corner is for conversations about the everyday work of running a business. Browse all the oases.

Post, reply, and follow your interests

Follow the oases you want to keep up with, then join a conversation whenever you have something to add. Start a post in the group that fits your topic, or leave a comment on someone else’s post. Reading along is fine, too.

Share a post with Google

When you write a post, you can choose whether to share that individual post with Google so people can find it in search results. Comments added after the post was shared may also be visible on Google. Keep that in mind when you reply to a shared post.

Get a free page for your local business

Local businesses can get a free Oasis19 profile with a web address like oasis19.com/b/your-business-name. It gives customers a place to learn what you do and see where you’re located or which area you serve. See the business directory.

Frequently asked questions

Does Oasis19 have ads?

No. Oasis19 has no ads and no algorithm that decides which conversations you see.

Can I follow more than one oasis?

Yes. You can follow the oases that match your interests, whether that’s football, cooking, or both.

Do I have to share my posts with Google?

No. You choose whether to share each individual post with Google.

Can a business without a storefront get a free page?

Yes. A service-area business can list the area it serves while keeping its street address private.

Where do I go to join the conversation?

Open the Oasis19 community app at oasis19.com/community/.
